Next Station: Budapest

Game Rules

How to Play

Controls:

  • Building Segments: Click on a station at the end of your line to select it, then click on another valid station to draw a metro segment between them
  • Draw Card: Click the "Draw Card" button to draw a new card without building
  • Skip Turn: Click "Skip Turn" to advance to the next turn without drawing or building

Quick Rules: Build straight lines (horizontal, vertical, or 45° diagonal) from the ends of your metro line to stations matching your current card letter.

Objective

Build Budapest's metro network across 4 lines to score the most points by connecting districts, crossing the Danube, and creating busy interchanges.

Game Flow

  • 4 Rounds - One for each metro line (M1/yellow, M2/red, M3/blue, M4/green)
  • Random Order - Line sequence is randomized each game
  • 8 Cards per Round - Draw station cards to build your line
  • Game ends after all 4 rounds are completed

Building Rules

First Segment

Must start from the line's designated starting station

Subsequent Segments

Must extend from either end of your existing line

Movement

Straight lines only: horizontal, vertical, or 45° diagonal

No Crossings

Segments cannot cross except at stations

No Revisiting

Cannot return to stations already on your line

No Parallels

Only one segment allowed between any two stations (across all rounds)

Station Cards

A, B, C, D Cards

Connect to stations with matching letters

Joker Cards (?)

Connect to any station type (also, Deák tér station is a wild/joker station)

Card Deck

The deck contains 10 cards: A, B, C, D, and ? (Joker).

Scoring System

Round Score (FP)

FP = (PK × PM) + PD

  • PK - Number of different districts touched by the line
  • PM - Maximum stations in any single district
  • PD - Number of Danube crossings (segments connecting Buda to Pest)

Final Bonuses

  • Train Stations (PP) - Points based on how many train stations you connected: 0→0pts, 1→1pt, 2→2pts, 3→4pts, 4→6pts, 5→8pts, 6→11pts, 7→14pts, 8→17pts, 9→21pts, 10+→25pts
  • Interchanges - Stations where multiple lines meet: 2 lines=2pts each, 3 lines=5pts each, 4 lines=9pts each

Final Score

Total Score = Sum(all round FP scores) + PP + (2 × P2) + (5 × P3) + (9 × P4)

Where:

  • Sum(FP) - Total of all 4 round scores
  • PP - Train station points from the sliding scale
  • P2 - Number of 2-line interchange stations
  • P3 - Number of 3-line interchange stations
  • P4 - Number of 4-line interchange stations
